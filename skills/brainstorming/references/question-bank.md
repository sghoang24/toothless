# Question Bank

Use to fill gaps in an unclear request. Ask only what is missing, **one question at a time** — wait for the answer before the next — and prefer concrete options over open-ended prompts when a decision has nameable choices. Never repeat back what the user already told you.

## Goal & Motivation

- What problem does this solve, and for whom?
- Why now? What happens if we don't build it?
- What does "done" look like to you?

## Scope

- What is the minimum version that is still useful?
- What should this explicitly NOT do?
- Are there parts that can be deferred to a later iteration?

## Acceptance Criteria

- How will we know it works? What will you test manually?
- What are the edge cases that must be handled?
- What errors should the user see, and when?

## Users & Scenario

- Who is the primary user (role, persona)?
- What is the typical usage flow, step by step?
- How often will this be used, and at what scale?

## Constraints

- Any required tech, libraries, or patterns to follow?
- Any hard deadlines, dependencies, or external systems?
- Security, privacy, performance, or compatibility requirements?
- Anything that must NOT change (interfaces, data, behavior)?

## Codebase Fit

- Have you seen similar functionality elsewhere in the project?
- Should this reuse an existing component/module or be new?
- Who owns or is affected by the area being modified?

## Approach (Brainstorming)

- Here are 2-3 approaches — which trade-off matters most: speed, quality, or flexibility?
- Any approach you already prefer or want to avoid? Why?
- What is the riskiest assumption here?
